An ordinary Tuesday offers very few opportunities to be remarkable. It offers
quite a few opportunities to be kind.

There is the message that deserves a thoughtful reply. The task nobody has
claimed. The person who needs a little more time to find the words. These things
are easy to miss when attention is already on the next large milestone.

## A smaller field of view

From a Christian perspective, love of neighbor has an inconveniently practical
quality. The neighbor is an actual person, with a name and a schedule and needs
that may not fit neatly into ours.

It is possible to think seriously about serving others while overlooking the
person standing in the kitchen. Good intentions still have to become something
concrete.

That might mean listening without rehearsing a reply. It might mean keeping a
small promise. It might mean doing the dull part of a shared responsibility
without making a production of it.

{{% callout title="Something to sit with" %}}
What would care look like here, with these people, in the time that is actually
available today?
{{% /callout %}}

## Enough for today

There is comfort in a task small enough to do. An entire life cannot be put in
order before dinner. A difficult conversation can be approached with patience.
A dish can be washed. An apology can be made.

Those actions do not resolve every larger question. They do give our convictions
a place to take root.

Tomorrow will have its own ordinary opportunities.
